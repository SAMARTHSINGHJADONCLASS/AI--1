Arduino Uno / Nano
ATmega328P Microcontroller running robot_hand_servos.ino. Receives UART commands at 9600 baud and generates smooth non-blocking PWM pulses on Pins 2..11.
Power: USB 5V | PWM Pins: 2..11 | Logic: 5V TTL
Servo Pin Mapping (PWM):
Thumb: Pins 2 & 3
Index: Pins 4 & 5
Middle: Pins 6 & 7
Ring: Pins 8 & 9
Pinky: Pins 10 & 11
Electrical Health Rules STABLE
1. External 5V 5A Supply: 10 servos draw up to 4A peak. Never power them from the Arduino 5V regulator pin!
2. Common Ground Tie: The external supply GND and Arduino GND must be connected together for reference.
3. Buffer Capacitor: 1000µF electrolytic capacitor absorbs inductive kickback during simultaneous motor starts.
